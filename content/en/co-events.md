    <h2>“One Student One Chip” and Tape-out-Ready Open-Source Chip Design Tutorial</h2>
    <p class="co-event-description">“One Student One Chip” (ysyx.org) was launched by the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2019 and has attracted more than 20,000 registrations from over 1,300 universities and research institutes worldwide. This tutorial focuses on ECOS Studio, an end-to-end open-source RISC-V chip design solution based on open-source EDA, IP, and PDK resources. It covers the complete RTL2GDSII design flow and includes guided hands-on use of ECOS Studio to advance RISC-V education and industry development.</p>
