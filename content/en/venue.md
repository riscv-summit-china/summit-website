transport_section_id: transport-guide
transport_section_title: Arrival Transportation Guide
transport_section_intro: Travel times and fares below are estimates under normal conditions. Please allow additional time during peak hours and the summit period.
transport_groups:
  - title: Shenzhen Bao’an International Airport → Venue
    routes:
      - title: Metro (recommended)
        description: Take Line 11 toward Futian to Chegongmiao Station, transfer to Line 1 toward Luohu, and exit from Exit D at Convention & Exhibition Center Station. The journey takes approximately 50–70 minutes and costs about RMB 7.
      - title: Taxi / ride-hailing
        description: The journey normally takes 30–45 minutes and costs approximately RMB 90–120. Set the destination to “Shenzhen Convention & Exhibition Center (Fuhua 3rd Road)”. Actual time and fare depend on traffic.
  - title: Hong Kong → Venue
    description: The following options are listed by recommendation and checkpoint convenience.
    routes:
      - title: High-speed rail (Hong Kong West Kowloon → Futian, recommended)
        description: The train takes approximately 14 minutes and uses co-located immigration clearance at West Kowloon Station. Arrive at least 45 minutes early for immigration and security. From Futian Station, walk about 1.5 km or 15 minutes along Yitian Road to the venue’s West Gate, or take a taxi for approximately 10 minutes.
        note: Tickets are available through 12306 and official MTR channels. Advance booking is recommended during the summit period.
      - title: Futian Checkpoint
        description: After immigration, take Metro Line 4 toward Niuhu directly to Convention & Exhibition Center Station and use Exit D. The metro journey takes approximately 12 minutes. Checkpoint hours are 06:30–22:30.
      - title: Luohu Checkpoint
        description: After immigration, take Metro Line 1 from Luohu Station directly to Convention & Exhibition Center Station and use Exit D. The metro journey takes approximately 20 minutes. Checkpoint hours are 06:30–22:30.
      - title: Huanggang Checkpoint
        description: Huanggang provides 24-hour immigration clearance. Take Metro Line 7 to Fumin Station, transfer to Line 4, and continue to Convention & Exhibition Center Station. The metro journey takes approximately 17 minutes.
      - title: Shekou Cruise Homeport
        description: After arriving by ferry and clearing immigration, take Metro Line 12 to Chegongmiao Station, transfer to Line 1, and use Exit D at Convention & Exhibition Center Station. The journey takes approximately 45–60 minutes; a taxi takes approximately 30–40 minutes.
        note: Ferry services are limited. Check schedules in advance and allow sufficient transfer time.
  - title: Domestic High-speed Rail Stations → Venue
    routes:
      - title: Shenzhen North Railway Station
        description: Take Metro Line 4 directly to Convention & Exhibition Center Station and use Exit D. The journey takes approximately 20 minutes.
      - title: Shenzhen Railway Station (Luohu)
        description: Take Metro Line 1 directly to Convention & Exhibition Center Station and use Exit D. The journey takes approximately 20 minutes.
admission_section_id: admission-parking
admission_section_title: Admission & Parking
admission_section_intro: The summit’s main activities are located on Floors 5–6 of Shenzhen Convention & Exhibition Center. Follow the route matching your arrival method.
admission_groups:
  - title: Driving & Parking
    recommendation: "Recommended route: underground parking → direct elevator to Floors 5–6"
    steps:
      - Navigate to “Shenzhen Convention & Exhibition Center P2 Underground Parking”. Enter through the North Plaza garage entrance on Fuhua 3rd Road or from the Binhai Boulevard service road travelling south to north.
      - From the car park, take Elevators 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 or 13 directly to Floor 5.
  - title: Coach & Taxi
    recommendation: "Recommended route: North Gate → North Plaza escalator → Floors 5–6"
    steps:
      - Alight at the North Gate and walk to the main entrance on the second-floor platform of the North Plaza.
      - After entering the second-floor lobby, take Elevators 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 or 13 directly to Floor 5.
dining_section_id: dining-guide
dining_section_title: Dining Guide
dining_section_intro: Dining options are concentrated around The Royal Plaza, Link Central City and COCO Park. They can be reached through the Link City underground mall from Exit B of the venue without crossing roads, which is convenient in rainy weather.
dining_groups:
  - title: Cantonese Cuisine & Dim Sum
    description: Nearby options include Diandude and Peninsula Grand Restaurant, serving classic Cantonese dishes such as shrimp dumplings, siu mai and crispy roasted pigeon. They are suitable for casual business meals and hospitality dinners, and selected restaurants provide English menus.
  - title: International Cuisine
    description: Western, Japanese, Korean and Southeast Asian restaurants are available at The Royal Plaza and Link Central City, catering to a range of dietary preferences.
  - title: Light Meals & Summit Working Meals
    description: Working-meal counters are located on Floor 1 of the venue. Meals may be redeemed with date-specific vouchers from October 17 to 20, 11:30–13:30 daily; vouchers are valid only on the printed date. Nearby options also include Chaoshan beef hot pot, pork tripe chicken soup and Hong Kong-style cafés.
    note: Link City underground commercial street offers a variety of light meals and coffee shops for quick dining during summit breaks.
notes_section_id: participant-notes
notes_section_title: Important Notes for Participants
participant_notes:
  - title: Badge & Document Management
    description: Overseas participants should carry their original passports. Participants from Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan should hold valid travel documents and endorsements. Imported articles must be declared in accordance with China Customs regulations.
  - title: Network Services
    description: Overseas mobile users may activate mainland China roaming in advance. Complimentary summit Wi-Fi is available inside the venue. A local SIM card may also be purchased for mobile connectivity and navigation.
  - title: Payment Methods
    description: WeChat Pay and Alipay are the most common payment methods in mainland China. Visa and Mastercard are widely accepted at hotels and shopping malls. Carrying a small amount of cash is recommended as a backup.
  - title: Border Crossing Reminder
    description: Land checkpoints are busiest from 08:00–10:00 and 17:00–20:00, and immigration queues may be longer during these periods. Please allow sufficient clearance time.
  - title: Meal Voucher Usage
    description: Meal vouchers are issued separately for October 17–20 and may only be redeemed on the printed date between 11:30 and 13:30 at the working-meal counters on Floor 1 of the venue. Expired vouchers will not be accepted.
guide_disclaimer: Information in this guide is for travel reference only. Travel time and border clearance efficiency are subject to passenger flow, road conditions and other factors. The Organizing Committee reserves the right to adjust the guide when necessary.
accommodation_guidance_title: Accommodation Selection Guide
accommodation_guidance:
  - title: Priority Areas
    description: Prioritize the Convention & Exhibition Center, Gangxia and Fumin areas. They are served by Metro Lines 1 and 4, within walking distance or a short metro ride of the venue, and provide convenient links to Hong Kong checkpoints.
  - title: Within Walking Distance
    description: Five-star hotels around the Futian CBD venue, including Four Seasons Hotel Shenzhen and Futian Shangri-La, are within approximately 10 minutes’ walk. They typically provide business centres and Chinese and Western dining, and are about 10 minutes by car from Futian Checkpoint.
  - title: One or Two Metro Stops Away
    description: Business-chain hotels near Gangxia and Fumin stations are approximately 2–5 minutes from the venue by metro, with many properties around a three-minute walk from a station. Supporting facilities are comprehensive, and Futian Checkpoint is around two metro stops away.
  - title: Budget Accommodation
    description: Shopping Park and Chegongmiao are one stop from the venue on Metro Line 1 and offer extensive dining with relatively affordable accommodation. Rooms are in high demand during the summit, so advance booking is recommended. For frequent Hong Kong travel, prioritize Futian Checkpoint or Fumin Station.
