transport_groups:
  - title: 1. Shenzhen Bao’an International Airport - Shenzhen Convention & Exhibition Center
    routes:
      - title: Metro (Recommended)
        description: Take Line 11 (Airport Line, towards Futian) to Chegongmiao Station, transfer to Line 1 (towards Luohu), and exit at Exit D of Convention & Exhibition Center Station. The whole journey takes approximately 50–70 minutes with a fare of about 7 RMB. Please allow ample travel time during rush hours.
      - title: Taxi / Ride-hailing
        description: "Estimated travel time: 30–45 minutes. Travel duration is subject to traffic conditions and will be longer during rush hours. Estimated fare: 90–120 RMB. Navigation destination: Shenzhen Convention & Exhibition Center (Fuhua 3rd Road)."
  - title: 2. Entry from Hong Kong to the Convention & Exhibition Center (Listed in Order of Priority)
    routes:
      - title: ① High-speed Rail (West Kowloon Station - Futian Station, Recommended Option)
        description: The ride from Hong Kong West Kowloon Station to Futian Station takes approximately 14 minutes, adopting the co-located immigration clearance arrangement. Both Hong Kong departure and mainland China entry inspections are completed at West Kowloon Station in one stop. Tickets can be purchased with a passport or Mainland Travel Permit for Hong Kong and Macao Residents. It is recommended to arrive at the station at least 45 minutes in advance for immigration clearance and security checks. After exiting Futian Station, walk along Yitian Road to the West Gate of the Convention & Exhibition Center. The distance is about 1.5 km, with a walking time of approximately 15 minutes. You may also take a taxi for the 10-minute ride.
        note: "Ticketing channels: 12306 platform and MTR official channels. Foreign nationals may buy tickets with their passports. Train services are expected to be tight during the exhibition period, advance booking is recommended."
      - title: ② Futian Port (the land port closest to the venue)
        description: 'Enter Shenzhen via Futian Port. Metro Line 4 "Futian Checkpoint Station" is located right outside the port exit. Take Line 4 (towards Niuhu) directly to Convention & Exhibition Center Station and exit via Exit D. The metro ride takes approximately 12 minutes. Port opening hours: 06:30–22:30.'
      - title: ③ Luohu Port
        description: 'Enter Shenzhen via Luohu Port. You will arrive at Metro Line 1 "Luohu Station". Take Line 1 directly to Convention & Exhibition Center Station and exit via Exit D. The metro ride takes approximately 20 minutes. Port opening hours: 06:30–22:30.'
      - title: ④ Huanggang Port
        description: Huanggang Port provides 24-hour immigration clearance, ideal for participants arriving in Shenzhen overnight. After entry, take Metro Line 7 to Fumin Station, transfer to Line 4 to get to Convention & Exhibition Center Station. The metro journey takes around 17 minutes.
      - title: ⑤ Shekou Cruise Homeport
        description: Take the ferry from Hong Kong to Shekou Cruise Homeport. After immigration clearance, you may travel to the venue by metro or taxi. Take Metro Line 12 (towards Zuopaotai East) to Chegongmiao Station, transfer to Line 1 (towards Luohu), and exit at Exit D of Convention & Exhibition Center Station. The whole journey takes approximately 45–60 minutes. You may also take a taxi; the estimated travel time is 30–40 minutes.
        note: Ferry services are limited. Please check the ferry schedule in advance and allow ample transfer time.
  - title: 3. Connecting Transportation from Domestic High Speed Rail Stations
    routes:
      - title: 1. Shenzhen North Railway Station
        description: Take Line 4 directly to Convention & Exhibition Center Station and exit via Exit D. The journey takes approximately 20 minutes.
      - title: 2. Shenzhen Station (Luohu)
        description: Take Line 1 directly to Convention & Exhibition Center Station and exit via Exit D. The journey takes approximately 20 minutes.

notes_section_id: participant-notes
notes_section_title: Important Notes for Participants
participant_notes:
  - title: 1. Badge & Document Management
    description: Overseas participants shall carry their original passports at all times. Participants from Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan shall hold valid travel documents with proper endorsements. Please declare imported articles in accordance with relevant regulations of China Customs.
  - title: 2. Network Services
    description: Overseas mobile users may activate roaming service for mainland China in advance. Complimentary summit-exclusive Wi-Fi is available within the venue. If local mobile connectivity is required, you may purchase a Chinese SIM card for travel navigation.
  - title: 3. Payment Methods
    description: WeChat Pay and Alipay are the mainstream payment methods in mainland China. Visa and Mastercard international credit cards are widely accepted at hotels and shopping malls. It is recommended to carry a small amount of cash for backup.
  - title: 4. Border Crossing Reminder
    description: Land border checkpoints experience peak passenger flow from 08:00–10:00 and 17:00–20:00, resulting in long waiting times for inspection. Please allow sufficient time for customs clearance.
  - title: 5. Meal Voucher Usage
    description: Meal vouchers are issued separately for each date from October 17 to 20. They can only be redeemed for working meals on the printed date between 11:30–13:30 at the 1st Floor of the venue, and will not be accepted after expiry.
guide_disclaimer: "* Information in this guide is for travel reference only. Travel duration and border clearance efficiency are subject to passenger flow, road conditions and other factors. The Organizing Committee reserves the right to modify the content of this guide."
accommodation_guidance_title: Where to Stay
accommodation_guidance_intro: Priority is given to accommodations in the Convention & Exhibition Center, Gangxia and Fumin areas. These locations are along Metro Lines 1 and 4, within walking distance or a short metro ride to the venue, and offer convenient transport links to Hong Kong border checkpoints.
accommodation_guidance:
  - title: 1. Within Walking Distance of the Venue (High-end Business, Priority for Overseas Guests)
    description: Five-star hotels in the CBD surrounding the Convention & Exhibition Center, including Four Seasons Hotel Shenzhen and Futian Shangri-La, Shenzhen. They are within a 10-minute walk to the venue and equipped with business centres, Chinese and Western catering services. It takes around 10 minutes by car to Futian Port, ideal for overseas guests and senior participants.
  - title: 2. Metro 1–2 Stops Away, Business Hotels (Recommended for Most Exhibitors)
    description: Business chain hotels near Gangxia and Fumin Metro Stations. It takes 2–5 minutes by metro to reach the Convention & Exhibition Center, with a roughly 3-minute walk to the metro stations. The hotels are fully equipped with supporting facilities. Some serve simple Chinese and Western meals. It is only two metro stops to Futian Port.
  - title: 3. Budget Accommodation
    description: Located in the Shopping Park and Chegongmiao areas, just one stop on Metro Line 1 to the Convention & Exhibition Center. The neighbourhood boasts abundant dining options with relatively affordable accommodation costs.
  - title: 4. Booking Reminder
    description: Accommodation is in high demand during the exhibition period. Advance reservations are recommended. If you need to travel frequently to and from Hong Kong, priority is given to accommodation near Futian Port and Fumin Metro Station.
